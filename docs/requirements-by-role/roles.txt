1. Buyers/Consumers
Highest priority:
- Real-time price & volume charts
- Advanced order search & filters
- Alerts & watchlists (price triggers, new product availability)
- Quick order initiation
- Order simulator/cost estimator
- Multi-language support
Secondary: 
- Market map, sustainability scorecards (for ESG reporting)

2. Sellers/Traders/Producers
Highest priority:
- Advanced order search to identify buyers & opportunities
- Alerts for demand changes
- Quick order posting & template reuse
- Localization/multi-language support
Secondary: 
- Analytics of buyer trends, sustainability metrics for marketing

3. Managers / Analysts (corporate users)
Highest priority:
- Market insights & analytics
- Data export/API access for external reporting
- Sustainability scorecards for ESG tracking
- Team collaboration & permission management
Secondary: 
- Interactive map for geographic strategy, onboarding & glossary tools for staff

4. Admin (Platform Operators)

Highest priority:
- Admin panel & user management
- Data quality management (orders, prices, sustainability metrics)
- Monitoring dashboard for KPIs (active users, CO₂ saved, trades executed)
- Ability to feature/approve new market categories or standards
Secondary: 
- Supporting user education content
