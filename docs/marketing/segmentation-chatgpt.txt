For a bio-circular-green trading platform, your potential users could include:

1. Farmers and Agricultural Producers: 
- They might want to trade bio-based resources like agricultural waste (biomass), biogas, or organic fertilizers.
2. Energy Producers: 
- Companies focused on renewable energy sources (e.g., biogas, biofuels) might trade bioenergy materials or resources.
3. Recyclers and Waste Management Firms:
- They can trade in circular economy materials like organic waste for composting or converting waste into energy.
4. Green Product Manufacturers: 
- Businesses producing eco-friendly, biodegradable, or recycled products may seek sustainable raw materials through your platform.
5. Sustainability Consultants/Organizations: 
- Organizations advocating for green policies, circular economy principles, or carbon reduction may also utilize your platform for sourcing bio-based materials.
6. Government Agencies and NGOs: 
- These entities may use your platform to promote sustainability initiatives or regulate bio and circular economy-related trade.
7. Eco-Conscious Consumers: 
- Individuals or groups looking to purchase sustainable, green, or circular economy products might engage directly in specific marketplaces.

Tailoring your platform to meet their needs will help attract these users.
