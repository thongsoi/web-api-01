Pallet 3 (Utopiaville)
1. Hopeful & Human-Centered (Recommended)
Primary: Soft teal or muted aqua (#4DB6AC or #5F9EA0)
→ Represents balance, clarity, and calm progress.
Secondary: Warm coral or peach (#FF7F50 or #FFB380)
→ Adds warmth, humanity, and energy without being aggressive.
Neutrals: Light gray or warm off-white (#F8F9FA or #F5F5DC) for backgrounds, dark charcoal (#333333) for text
→ Ensures readability and accessibility.
2. Progressive & Trustworthy
Primary: Deep green (#2E7D32 or #388E3C)
→ Symbolizes growth, sustainability, and shared prosperity.
Accent: Golden yellow or amber (#FFC107 or #FFB300)
→ Evokes optimism, opportunity, and economic security.
Neutrals: Clean white (#FFFFFF) and soft gray (#E0E0E0)
3. Inclusive & Calm
Primary: Gentle blue (#64B5F6 or #42A5F5)
→ Conveys trust, stability, and fairness.
Accent: Lavender or soft purple (#9575CD or #B39DDB)
→ Suggests innovation and social progress.
Neutrals: Off-white and light taupe


Pallet 4 (BiomassX)
Core Color Palette
Forest Green (#2E5A32 or #2D5016)
Represents nature, growth, and renewable resources.
Signals environmental responsibility and ties directly to biomass sources like wood, crops, and organic matter.
Earth Brown (#5C4033 or #6B4423)
Evokes soil, wood, and raw organic materials—core components of biomass.
Adds warmth and grounding to the design.
Clean White or Light Beige (#F8F9FA or #F5F0E6)
Provides contrast and readability.
Light beige subtly reinforces the natural/organic feel without being stark.
Accent: Energy Gold or Amber (#D4A017 or #FFA726)
Symbolizes energy, warmth, and the transformation of raw biomass into usable fuel.
Draws attention to calls-to-action (e.g., “Trade Now,” “List Product”).
Supporting Cool Tone: Deep Teal or Blue-Green (#1B6C60 or #00796B)
Suggests clean technology, efficiency, and innovation in bioenergy processing.
Balances the warmth of greens and browns with a modern, technical feel.
