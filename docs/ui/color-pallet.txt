| (2) Alert / Warning**                 | `#F59E0B` (amber)                                                | Non-critical warnings or trending changes.                                                                 |
| **Error / Negative**                  | `#EF4444` (red 500)                                              | Clear negative signals or failed transactions.                                                             |
